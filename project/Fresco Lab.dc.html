Round 03 · Fresco, with your direction

The Chart

Cards are zodiac signs. Signs carry elements. Elements agree or fight with their neighbours on the board. Your Jokers are the Major Arcana. The chain you liked is still here — it just became something you can see, spread across a grid instead of ticking in a corner.

FIRST, YOUR OBJECTION

Fresco is not hard to build. It is Carcassonne.

You feared it because "the illustration is the mechanic" sounds like the computer has to understand pictures. It does not. The computer compares two symbols; the illustration is what makes the match obvious to a human before they have read anything. Carcassonne has shipped this exact model since 2000, in cardboard.

Here is the entire data model of the prototype below. Every rule in the game is one of these five lines. If your artist draws Aries so that its fire visibly licks toward the edge of the card, the player will feel continuity the engine never computed.

// the whole game
Sign = { glyph, element, modality }
Cell = Sign | empty
Board = Cell[16]
// the only rule that matters
aspect(a, b) =
a == b → conjunction
compatible(a, b) → trine
else → square
