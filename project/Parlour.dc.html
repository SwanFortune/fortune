Round 04 · The concept

The Parlour

It's poker, but the hands are horoscopes — and you're bluffing the client sitting across the table.

You are a fraud in a witch's den. Clients sit down, each with a price on their belief. You deal spreads of zodiac cards; named combinations — Conjunction, Trine, Grand Cross — score like poker hands. Tarot Arcana up your sleeve break the rules. And the client watches, and interrupts, and does not care for water signs.

THE BORROWED VOCABULARY

Balatro works because you already know what a Full House is. Astrology is the only other scoring vocabulary a stranger walks in already half-knowing. Conjunction is a pair. Trine is a flush. Grand Cross is a straight. Nobody needs a tutorial for this, and that is the whole reason to pick it.

THE SCORE IS DIEGETIC

You are not producing chips. You are producing Awe, and the quota is a person's Doubt. Same arithmetic as Balatro, but every number on screen is a thing happening in the fiction. That costs zero engineering and buys you the entire tone of the game.

THE ENEMY IS A FACE

Each client is a portrait, a demand, one rule they impose, and three lines of dialogue. No combat, no HP, no AI — but a person reacting to your spread lands harder than a health bar. One illustration and four strings per enemy. That is the cheapest personality in games.
